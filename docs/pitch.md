# Pitch: the 3-minute video

One story: a Powercoders job coach finds the IT jobs a graduate can start in, with a source for every number. Six scenes, 3 minutes, a screen recording with voice. Proposal, open for Isabella's feedback.

**The one sentence.** AareRadar shows Powercoders' team, in plain language, where the Swiss IT market has jobs a beginner can start in, which skills those jobs ask for and who hires, with the source and date next to every number.

**Who speaks.** One voice over a screen recording of radar.gnamak.ch at 1280 px, in the look the team picks. Short sentences, no technical words.

1. **0:00 to 0:20, the problem.** «Powercoders has placed 423 graduates; 70 percent found a job. But the market moves: which IT jobs take beginners right now, and where? Today that knowledge is spread over job boards, federal statistics and parliament.» Screen: the start page, the AareRadar sign turning.
2. **0:20 to 0:40, who it is for.** «AareRadar is for the people who prepare graduates for work: programme managers, company relations and the volunteer job coaches. No technical knowledge needed: every chart says in one sentence what it means, and names its source and date.» Screen: point at a «What this means» line and a source line.
3. **0:40 to 1:50, the demo, entry level first.** Five short stops, about 14 seconds each:
- **A Demand:** «3,150 IT ads in the last 60 days. Here are the IT jobs that are open to beginners: system engineer, software engineer, IT support.» Screen: the beginner ranking.
- **B Skills:** «What do these ads ask for? This tells the team what to teach the next cohort.»
- **C Employers:** «Who hires, without recruiting agencies: a list of companies to approach for internships.»
- **D Target group:** «How many refugees with status F, B and S work, by canton, from the State Secretariat for Migration.»
- **E Policy:** «New items in parliament and federal law on asylum, integration and work permits.»
4. **1:50 to 2:20, built to last.** «The data refreshes itself every day. Every two weeks the team gets a short email with the new beginner ads by category. And a new source is one line in a list, no developer needed.» Screen: the sources page.
5. **2:20 to 2:45, the rules we kept.** «Only official and open data: job-room.ch of SECO, the Federal Statistical Office, the State Secretariat for Migration, the federal parliament. No scraping of LinkedIn or jobs.ch, no personal data, only counts.»
6. **2:45 to 3:00, close.** «AareRadar: so the next Powercoders graduate starts where the jobs are.» Screen: the wordmark.

**Recording tips.** Rehearse once with a timer. Record the screen first, then the voice. Open the pages beforehand so nothing loads on camera. Use the English page. Hide the «Design options» button for the recording by choosing the look first, then reload with the link.

**Facts used, with sources.** 423 graduates, 89 percent internship, 70 percent job, 242 partner companies: powercoders.org/impact. 3,150 IT ads: job-room.ch, last 60 days, counted today. The numbers on screen come from the live page on the day of recording; check them before you say them.
