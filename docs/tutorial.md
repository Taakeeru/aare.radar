# How the page works: a short tutorial

Five things to know: the menu A to E, the role buttons, the «What this means» line, the source line under every number, and the «Design options» button. Plus a 90-second demo path for the video.

Open **radar.gnamak.ch/en** (English) or **/de** (German). The switch top right changes the language on the same page.

1. **The menu A to E.** Each letter is one question from the Powercoders challenge: **A Demand** (is there work?), **B Skills** (what do ads ask for?), **C Employers** (who hires?), **D Target group** (refugees and migrants in work), **E Policy** (what parliament and the law change). **Sources** lists every data source with its last update.
2. **The role buttons.** On A, B and C you choose a role: Web developer, IT support or Data analyst. Every number on the page then is for that role. The new panels under A show **all IT jobs** and which of them are **open to beginners**.
3. **«What this means».** Under each chart one plain sentence says what the numbers mean. Read that line first; it is written for people without a data background.
4. **The source line.** Under every number: where it comes from (job-room.ch of SECO, Federal Statistical Office, State Secretariat for Migration, parliament) and the date of the data. A click opens the source.
5. **«Design options» bottom right.** Try background, accent colour, font and the written name. «Copy link» gives a link to exactly that look, so you can send it to the team or use it for the video.

**Good to know**
- **Fresh data:** job ads every morning at 06:17; statistics when the office publishes (quarterly or monthly).
- **Only counts:** no names, no ad texts are kept. An ad is read once to count skills and hurdles, then dropped.
- **Beginner ads:** an ad counts as open to beginners when it says junior, trainee, internship, career changer or similar, or the employer marked no experience or less than a year.

**A 90-second demo path for the video**
1. Start page, English, in the chosen look. Say the one sentence.
2. **A Demand:** the total of IT ads, then «Which IT jobs are open to beginners».
3. Click **IT support** in the role buttons: the numbers change with it.
4. **B Skills:** the top skills for that role.
5. **C Employers:** companies hiring, without recruiting agencies.
6. **D Target group:** one chart on employment of people with status F or S.
7. **E Policy:** the newest items from parliament.
8. **Sources:** every source with its date, then back to the start page.

Tip: open all pages once before recording, so nothing loads on camera.
